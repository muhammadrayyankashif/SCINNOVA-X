FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can we register as private candidates?

+

Yes, private delegations and independent student teams from any institution or city across Pakistan are fully eligible to register.

What is the difference between the 4-Module and 6-Module packages?

+

The packages determine the number of events your team is eligible to contest. The 4-Module Package allows participation in up to four specific competition categories, while the 6-Module Package offers broader access, allowing teams to compete across six diverse categories for maximum points.

Do early bird registrants get specific benefits?

+

Teams applying in the Early Bird Round receive priority slot confirmation and preference in module selections before capacity limits are reached.

Is it possible to pay the fees in cash?

+

Teams must submit payments via online bank transfer or direct deposit to the official Cedar College designated account (Faysal Bank) and attach their proof of payment in the registration form.

What is the refund policy?

+

Registration fees are non-refundable and non-transferable once payment processing and team credentials have been verified.
