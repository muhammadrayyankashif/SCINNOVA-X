A DECADE OF
INNOVATION.

SCINNOVA is Cedar College’s flagship national science olympiad—engineered to push beyond conventional classroom theory. Now entering its landmark tenth edition, SCINNOVA X brings together the sharpest young scientific minds from across Karachi and Pakistan for three days of intense competition.

Featuring nine rigorous modules spanning theoretical physics, algorithmic computing, applied engineering, and forensic science, SCINNOVA X challenges delegates to solve high-stakes problems with precision, speed, and critical intellect. Doomsday is coming—only the prepared will prevail.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Yes, private delegations and independent student teams from any institution or city across Pakistan are fully eligible to register.

Teams applying in the Early Bird Round receive priority slot confirmation and preference in module selections before capacity limits are reached.

Teams must submit payments via online bank transfer or direct deposit to the official Cedar College designated account (Faysal Bank) and attach their proof of payment in the registration form.

Registration fees are non-refundable and non-transferable once payment processing and team credentials have been verified.
