ABOUT
CEDAR.

Cedar College is a Cambridge International A-Level institution in Karachi, operating across two purpose-built campuses in DHA Phase VIII and PECHS for over a decade.

Alongside rigorous A-Level academics supported by its Liberal Arts Core, Peer Tutoring, and Teaching Assistant programs, campus life revolves around the Cedar Enrichment Programme (CEP)—more than 21 student-led societies spanning Cedar National Model United Nations (1,100+ delegates), the Cedar College Debating Championship, Cedar Evening Bazaar (3,000+ visitors), Cedar Care community outreach, the arts, and STEM.

ABOUT
SCINNOVA.

Founded in 2017, SCINNOVA is Cedar College’s flagship three-day national STEM olympiad, returning on 8 9 10 January 2027 for its landmark tenth edition. Across 100+ participating schools and colleges and a wider community reach of 4,500+, delegations gather in Karachi for applied scientific problem-solving under timed competition pressure.

Delegations of 4 to 7 members aged 14–18 compete across nine interdisciplinary modules spanning mechanical engineering, laboratory sciences, mathematics, astronomy, computer science, psychology, and forensics. Each module runs two competitive rounds over three hours of active execution.

FIVE SOCIETIES.
ONE MISSION.

  1. @crew.cedar ↗

    CREW

    Cedar’s Robotics and Engineering Wing—winners of the 2025 International 4×4 Robotics Competition in Abu Dhabi, UAE. Outside SCINNOVA, CREW organizes Circuit Wars (featuring Sail the Circuit telemetry watercraft), builds campus installations like Mechanical Muses and Laser Maze, and engineered the 3D-printed electronics behind Project Polaris. Leads Planck’s Paradox and Automaton.

  2. @cords.cedar ↗

    CoRDS

    Council of Research and Development of Sciences—Cedar’s student research and life-sciences society, backed by a published student research paper. CoRDS stages Evidentia (Karachi’s 150+ participant forensics competition), leads FRIP First Aid training for 50+ students across both campuses, coordinates university laboratory visits, and leads Asclepius, Illuminatio, and Freud’s Forsit.

  3. @juno.cedar ↗

    Juno Circle

    Creators of Project Sky (65,786 ft stratosphere launch) and six-month co-builders of Project Polaris—a custom star tracker accurate to 15 arcseconds of periodic error used to image the Andromeda Galaxy and Rosette Nebula from Gorakh Hill. Also hosts Equinox, Cedar’s intraschool math and astrophysics olympiad, and leads Ptolemy’s Puzzles and Redshift.

  4. @cedar.codes ↗

    Cedar CODES

    Organizers and hosts of DayDream Karachi in partnership with Hack Club, bringing together 400+ student developers alongside sprint events such as Hackpocalypse. Builds SCINNOVA’s algorithmic, cryptography, and software challenges and leads Qwerty across both campuses.

  5. @cedarmindsports ↗

    MindSports

    Organizers of Cognivia—Pakistan’s only college-level FIDE-rated rapid chess tournament featuring 80+ players from 15+ institutes across U-16 and U-20 divisions—plus Logikon (two days of chess, Scrabble, and speedcubing) and the 2025 National Science Bowl Pakistan Karachi Regional Runners-Up squad. Leads Arcanum.
