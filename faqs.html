HOW TO REGISTER

The early registration deadline is 29 November.

The event will be held at Cedar College, DHA Campus.

Participants can select 4–6 modules, with Arcanum being compulsory.

A dedicated WhatsApp group is available where participants can connect with other interested students and find potential team members.

Once you have successfully submitted the registration form, you will receive a confirmation email within 2–3 days.

Students from Grade 9 to A2 (or equivalent) are eligible to participate.

No, the registration fee remains the same regardless of the number of modules selected by a team.

No, there is no specific syllabus for the modules. The challenges are primarily designed to assess participants' skills, critical thinking, and problem-solving abilities, ensuring fairness across all grade levels.

The Early Bird registration fee is PKR 4,000 per delegate, with no delegation fee. The Standard registration fee is PKR 4,500 per delegate, along with a PKR 2,000 delegation fee.

Yes, all registered participants will receive a certificate of participation.

Each delegation must consist of a minimum of 4 members and a maximum of 7 members.

Yes, students may register independently as private participants without representing a school.

Payments must be made through an online bank transfer or direct deposit into the official Faysal Bank account designated by Cedar College. Proof of payment must be attached to the registration form.

No, registration fees are strictly non-refundable and non-transferable once payment processing and team credentials have been verified.
