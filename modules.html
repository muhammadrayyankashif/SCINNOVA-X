SCINNOVA X / TENTH EDITION

THE MODULES

  1. 01

    Mystery

    Arcanum

    Arcanum blends science with mystery through forensic science, data encryption, and logic puzzles. Teams tackle cryptographic challenges and crime scene analysis, developing deductive reasoning skills to uncover hidden patterns.

  2. 02

    Chemistry

    Illuminatio

    In Illuminatio, students engage in hands-on chemical experiments, investigating molecular reactions, kinetics, and energy transformations. The module emphasizes precision laboratory protocol and rigorous application of the scientific method.

  3. 03

    Mathematics

    Ptolemy’s Puzzle

    Named after the famed polymath, this module demands high-speed mathematical modeling, geometric deduction, and analytical puzzles. Students apply advanced algebra, geometry, and calculus to resolve abstract and applied scenarios.

  4. 04

    Physics

    Planck’s Paradox

    Theoretical physics meets real-world mechanics. From quantum states to relativistic phenomena, students grapple with paradoxes that challenge conventional intuition and test their grasp of the fundamental laws of our universe.

  5. 05

    Biology

    Asclepius

    An immersive journey through life sciences, genetics, and anatomical analysis. Participants navigate clinical case studies and biological simulations, decoding the intricate mechanisms sustaining living systems.

  6. 06

    Astronomy

    Redshift

    Exploring astrophysics and deep space cosmology. Delegations evaluate telescopic datasets, stellar spectra, and gravitational models, applying mathematical astrophysics to decipher celestial phenomena.

  7. 07

    Robotics

    Automaton

    Applied mechatronics, circuit architecture, and mechanical engineering. Teams build, program, and pilot hardware systems under real-time constraint arenas, testing autonomous control and sensor integration.

  8. 08

    Psychology

    Freud’s Forsit

    Investigating cognitive functions, behavioral economics, and decision biases. Delegates design psychological experiments and evaluate neurological responses to dissect the complexities of human cognition.

  9. 09

    Computer Science

    QWERTY

    Algorithmic problem-solving, data structures, and computational optimization. Teams tackle timed competitive programming challenges and system architecture questions that demand algorithmic efficiency.
